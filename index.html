My Biography

My name is Diphete Kay Matlanyane. I come from Ga-Masemola, Sekhukhune, in Limpopo Province, South Africa.

I studied at two education campuses, where I obtained my Diploma in Office Management. My educational background has helped me develop an interest in administration, organisation and professional service.

I am passionate about entrepreneurship, business development and creating opportunities through hard work, determination and dedication.

Through Tlhantlhagane Ya Bokone Investment Pty Ltd, I aim to grow a business that serves customers, supports local enterprise and explores opportunities in indigenous tea, general trading and supply.

My motto: The Power of Hustling.
My vision is to keep learning, build a sustainable business and contribute positively to my community.
