Who We Are

About Our Company

Our Company

TLHANTLHAGANE YA BOKONE INVESTMENT PTY LTD is a South African company established with an ambition to grow into a diversified and sustainable business.

Our business interests include indigenous tea manufacturing and sales, general trading, supply services and other business opportunities.

We aim to build lasting relationships through commitment, professionalism, dependable service and a strong work ethic.

Our Background

Our journey is inspired by family heritage, entrepreneurship and the belief that a small beginning can lead to a meaningful future.

Indigenous tea is an important part of our business story. We aim to preserve traditional knowledge while developing products for a wider market.

From our roots in Ga-Masemola, Limpopo, we aspire to expand our reach and establish strong business relationships across South Africa.

The Person Behind The Vision

Our Founder and Director

Diphete Kay Matlanyane

Diphete Kay Matlanyane is the Founder and Director of TLHANTLHAGANE YA BOKONE INVESTMENT PTY LTD. Originally from Mahlakole Section, Ga-Masemola, in Limpopo Province, South Africa, the founder's journey reflects determination, education, family heritage and an entrepreneurial spirit.

The founder studied for a Diploma in Office Management and Technology at Central University of Technology and obtained an N6 Certificate in Management Assistant at Goldfields TVET College, Welkom Campus, in the Free State.

The company's tea journey is rooted in family tradition. The founder's father prepared indigenous tea using selected bush trees. By helping with this traditional activity and selling the tea, the founder developed a passion for preserving indigenous knowledge and building a business from this heritage.

The long-term ambition is to develop a respected enterprise that creates employment, supports communities, supplies customers and contributes to economic development.
