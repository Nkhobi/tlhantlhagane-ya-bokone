Welcome to Our Company

TLHANTLHAGANE YA BOKONE INVESTMENT PTY LTD is a South African business based in Ga-Masemola, Limpopo Province. Our business interests include indigenous tea, tea manufacturing and sales, general trading, and supply services.

Our vision is to build a trusted business that celebrates indigenous products, supports enterprise, and serves our customers professionally.
